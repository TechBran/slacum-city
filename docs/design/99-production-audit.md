## 1. The headline

At `fd4d8a0` the tree is green — 2,234 tests, half a million asserts, zero failures — and the game on the phone is not the game in the documents. The simulation computes what docs 01–10 say it computes and the tests prove it; what nothing proves is the seam between a computed value and its **consequence** or its **surface**, and the seven lenses found that seam open in roughly thirty places. Five of them are Critical on the path a player actually plays: standing water closes and slows nothing while the push copy says traffic is being turned back (PA-01); a green placement ghost will put a 90 kW high-rise on a 50 kVA transformer and punish the player with a burnout hours later (PA-02); every cold launch — a swipe from recents, a reboot, an overnight kill — resumes the city frozen at the pause with no catch-up and no away report (PA-03); the Disaster Director stalls after its first two minor events in every save, so the authored thunderstorm never comes (PA-04); and the building panel's `Fix this →` on its most common blocker looks a transformer id up in `sim.buildings` and returns (PA-05). Below those sit twenty Highs of the same shape — a warning event authored and never emitted, a reclose that never resolves, a graphics preset that changes vehicle counts and nothing else, a water outage that moves nothing but revenue, a `building_rules.json` the validator approves and no gate reads. **None of this is a tuning problem, and the retunes in flight (decay, income, upgrade prices, the power model) will land on top of it and change none of it** — one of them (the decay retune) will silently change nothing at all unless it also lands the readers PA-13 names. Verdict: **not shippable to strangers at this fork**; shippable to the owner's own Fold for the loops that exist. The five Criticals and twenty of the Highs are each a Medium-effort lane or less; §3 groups the survivors into 19 single-agent lanes that do not share a file, and the cheapest lane in it (Q, the gates) is the one that stops the pattern recurring — every P0 here is A91-D-19's shape again, and A91-D-19 was closed by a census, not by a fix.

### 1.2 How to read a row

- **id** — `PA-nn`, rank order inside its severity band; `· A91-D-nn` when the row is promoted to doc 91 §14.5 (RR-125 says which rows are).
- **sev** — P0…P3 as RR-123 defines them: **P0** the shipped build lies to the player, discards their progress or a valid decision, or a constitution-level loop is unreachable in every save; **P1** an authored mechanic doc 91 grades SHIPPED cannot fire or be reached, or a control lies about part of what it claims; **P2** a drift or missing surface the player can route around, or a test gap with a named on-device consequence; **P3** doc-only, cosmetic or forward-looking.
- **evidence** — commands and `file:line` at `fd4d8a0`. Re-run the command; do not re-read the sentence (RR-94(e)).
- **effort** — S ≤ half a day, M ≤ two days, L more, tests included.
- **lens** — SHAPE (A91-D-19 shape hunter: keys, verbs, seams, events, settings), LOOP (player-loop critic), NEW (can a new player do it), VISUAL (the visual bar), PHONE (does it survive the phone), SIM (does the simulation do what the docs say), BREAK (where will it break next). Two names means two lenses found the same defect and the row cites both.
- **in flight** — the row, or the half named, belongs to a Wave-17 lane and is recorded here so the merge can be checked against it (RR-124); it is not proposed again in §3.
